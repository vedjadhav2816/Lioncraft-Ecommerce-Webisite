Accessibility Commitment

Website Accessibility

We strive to make our website accessible to all users, adhering to WCAG 2.1 guidelines. Features include keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, and adjustable text sizes.

Product Accessibility

Our products, including t-shirts and accessories, are designed with inclusivity in mind, offering a range of sizes and easy-to-read care labels for diverse needs.

Customer Support

We provide accessible customer support through multiple channels, including email, phone, and live chat, with accommodations for users with disabilities.

Feedback and Improvements

We welcome feedback on our accessibility efforts. Contact us at accessibility@lioncraft.com to report issues or suggest improvements.

Training and Awareness

Our team receives regular training on accessibility best practices to ensure we maintain an inclusive experience for all customers.

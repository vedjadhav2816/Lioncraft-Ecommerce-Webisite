Cookie Policy

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us enhance your browsing experience, such as remembering your cart items or preferences.

Types of Cookies We Use

We use essential cookies for website functionality (e.g., session management), performance cookies to analyze site usage, and marketing cookies to deliver personalized ads (if you consent).

How We Use Cookies

Cookies enable us to process orders, improve website performance, and provide tailored content. For example, we use cookies to keep your cart active during your visit.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies via our cookie consent banner or your browser settings. Disabling cookies may limit some website features, like cart functionality.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services (e.g., Google Analytics, advertising partners) that set cookies to analyze traffic or serve ads. These are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Contact Us

For questions about our Cookie Policy, contact us at privacy@lioncraft.com or through our customer support portal.

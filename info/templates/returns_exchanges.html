🔄 Returns & Exchanges

At LionCraft, we want you to love your purchase. If something isn’t right, we’ve got you covered with our hassle-free returns and exchanges policy.

Our Promise

We offer a 30-day return window for all items purchased from LionCraft. If you’re not completely satisfied, you can return or exchange your item with ease.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and in the same condition as received.
  • Proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is required.
  • Customized or personalized items are non-returnable unless defective.
  • Returns must be initiated within 30 days of delivery.

How to Return or Exchange

  1. Contact our support team at support@lioncraft.com with your order number.
  2. We’ll provide a return shipping label and instructions.
  3. Pack the item securely and drop it off at the nearest shipping point.
  4. Once received, we’ll process your refund or exchange within 5–7 business days.

Refunds

Refunds will be issued to your original payment method. Shipping costs are non-refundable, and a small restocking fee may apply for non-defective returns.

Need Help?

We’re here for you! Reach out to us at support@lioncraft.com or call us at +1 (234) 567-890.
